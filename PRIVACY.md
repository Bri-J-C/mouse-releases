# Mouse Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 7, 2026_

Mouse is a remote touchpad and keyboard: an Android app that controls the
pointer and keyboard of your own computers, together with a small host program
that runs on those computers.

## What Mouse collects

**Nothing.** Mouse has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or
tracking of any kind, and it does not send any data to the developer or to any
third party.

## What Mouse does with data on your devices

- **Local network only, encrypted.** The app communicates directly with Mouse
  host programs on your local network, and that traffic is encrypted
  (AES-256-GCM). Pointer movements, clicks, scrolling and
  typed keys go only to the computer you are controlling and are never sent
  to the internet.
- **Typed text is not stored.** Keystrokes are passed to your computer as you
  type them and are not saved on the phone or the computer.
- **Pairing data stays on your devices.** When you pair, your phone and your
  computer each store a random pairing key so the phone can reconnect without
  the PIN. The phone also remembers the names and local network addresses of
  computers you have used, and your settings (pointer speed, scrolling and so
  on). This stays on your devices and is deleted when you uninstall the app or
  delete a computer from its host list.
- **Text-field detection (Windows).** While your phone is connected, the
  Windows host checks whether the focused control on your computer accepts
  text, so the phone can show its keyboard. Only "a text field is focused: yes
  or no" is sent to your phone. No screen content or text is read or sent.

## Permissions

The Android app uses network access (to talk to your computers), Wi-Fi state
(to find computers on your network) and a Wi-Fi lock (to keep the connection
responsive while the app is open). It does not use location, contacts, the
camera, the microphone or storage.

## Children

Mouse is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone.
